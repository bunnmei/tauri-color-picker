Features

Pick colors across apps

Click anywhere on screen with the eyedropper — design tools, browsers, anything.

Copy as HEX / RGB / HSL

Choose the format and whether to wrap it in ' or ", then paste it straight into CSS or code.

History and palette

Your last 6 colors are kept. Drag and drop them onto the palette to collect a color scheme.

Light / dark

Switch themes with one button, and change the palette background to check colors against a real backdrop.
